# Problems and Solutions of the Slices Platform

---

## 1. Problems of traditional financing

### Difficulty of access to real-world asset investments

**Problem:** Investments in real-world assets — real estate, businesses, equipment — are traditionally available only to accredited investors and institutional funds. Retail investors have practically no opportunity to participate in such projects because of regulatory and legal barriers.

**How Slices solves it:** The platform tokenizes real-world assets through smart contracts (ERC1155), turning them into digital shares — RWA tokens. Any user can buy a share in a pool that represents a real asset without needing accredited investor status. The process is fully automated: AI risk evaluation, multi-signature approval, and smart contract deployment take minutes.

**Key benefits:**
- Democratization of access — anyone can participate
- Automated onboarding through the AI Risk Evaluator
- Transparent tokenization on the BSC blockchain

---

### High entry thresholds

**Problem:** Traditional investments in real estate or a business require large capital — from hundreds of thousands to millions of dollars. This excludes most retail investors.

**How Slices solves it:** The platform allows splitting any asset into many tokens. An investor can buy one or more shares (RWA tokens) in a pool instead of the entire asset. The minimum entry amount is determined by the parameters of a specific pool and can be very low.

**Key benefits:**
- Splitting assets into tokens of any denomination
- Low entry threshold for retail investors
- Ability to diversify across many pools

---

### Asset illiquidity

**Problem:** Traditional investments in real-world assets are hard to sell before maturity. Real estate can take months to sell, a business share can take years. The investor is effectively "locked" into the investment until it completes.

**How Slices solves it:** The platform provides a built-in Automated Market Maker (AMM) — a decentralized exchange where participants can buy and sell RWA tokens at any time. The AMM uses HOLD token and RWA token reserves to provide liquidity, and prices are determined algorithmically based on the ratio of the reserves.

**Key benefits:**
- Exit an investment at any time through the AMM
- Algorithmic pricing without intermediaries
- Constant pool liquidity

---

### Lack of transparency

**Problem:** In traditional investments, minority investors often have no access to detailed information about the asset, the cash flows, and the deal terms. All information is held by intermediaries.

**How Slices solves it:** All pool parameters — target amount, fees, tranche schedules (incoming/outgoing), AMM reserves — are stored in smart contracts on the BSC blockchain and are publicly verifiable. The AI Risk Evaluator assigns each business a numeric risk score (1–100), which is also available to all participants.

**Key benefits:**
- Full transparency through the blockchain
- Public risk score from the AI
- Verifiable smart contracts

---

### How Slices solves these problems (summary)

| Problem | Slices solution |
|----------|----------------|
| Difficulty of access | RWA tokenization through ERC1155, open to everyone |
| High entry thresholds | Splitting assets into tokens |
| Illiquidity | Built-in AMM for instant trading |
| Lack of transparency | All data on the blockchain, AI scoring |
| High fees | Automation through smart contracts, no intermediaries |

## 2. Crowdfunding problems

### Trust between the creator and investors

**Problem:** In classic crowdfunding (Kickstarter, GoFundMe), backers trust the project creator "at their word". There is no guarantee that the funds raised will be used as intended or that the promised rewards will be paid out. Fraudulent projects are a common occurrence.

**How Slices solves it:** The platform uses a multi-layered protection system:
- **AI Risk Evaluator** automatically assesses every business and pool, assigning a risk score based on the description, business type, documents, and images
- **Multi-signature approval (multi-sig)** — deploying the RWA token and pool requires signatures from several authorized signers
- **Incoming tranches** — the schedule of expected returns from the business is hard-coded into the smart contract; if the business does not return funds on time, this is recorded on the blockchain

**Key benefits:**
- Automatic AI risk assessment of every project
- Multi-sig prevents fraudulent deployment
- Smart contracts guarantee the fulfillment of obligations

---

### Complexity of legal formalities

**Problem:** Traditional crowdfunding requires complex legal formalities: contracts, licenses, regulatory approvals. For small businesses and startups, this is expensive and slow.

**How Slices solves it:** The platform automates the legal and procedural aspects through smart contracts:
- **Outgoing tranches** — funds are distributed to the business on a schedule hard-coded into the contract rather than as a lump sum
- **AddressBook with RBAC** — role separation (Admin, Manager, Signer, ProtocolContract) with clear permissions
- **Timelock** — a 2-day delay for critical operations (Treasury withdrawal, unfreezing)

**Key benefits:**
- Minimized legal costs
- Automatic distribution of funds in tranches
- Role-based security model

---

### No secondary market

**Problem:** In classic crowdfunding, backers cannot sell their share before the project completes. Funds are frozen for the entire campaign period, which puts off many potential participants.

**How Slices solves it:** The platform provides a full-fledged secondary market through the **AMM (Automated Market Maker)**. Participants can:
- Sell RWA tokens at any time through the AMM pool
- Buy additional tokens from other participants
- Use algorithmic pricing without intermediaries

**Key benefits:**
- Instant exit from an investment
- Constant liquidity
- Market-based pricing

---

### How Slices solves these problems (summary)

| Crowdfunding problem | Slices solution |
|------------------------|----------------|
| Trust and fraud | AI Risk Evaluator + multi-sig + incoming tranches |
| Legal complexity | Automation through smart contracts + RBAC |
| No secondary market | Built-in AMM for trading RWA tokens |
| No refunds | Incoming tranches with blockchain recording |
| No project scoring | AI risk assessment (1–100) |
| No share tokenization | ERC1155 tokens representing a share in a pool |

## 3. Governance problems

### Centralization

**Problem:** Traditional financial platforms are run by a single company or group of people who can unilaterally change the rules, fees, and terms. This creates a risk of abuse and a single point of failure.

**How Slices solves it:** The platform uses a **DAO (Decentralized Autonomous Organization)**:
- Key protocol parameters (fees, limits, periods) are controlled through voting by PlatformToken stakers
- **Role separation (RBAC)** through the AddressBook: Governance, Timelock, UpgradeRole, Signers, ProtocolContract — each role has strictly limited permissions
- **Emergency Pause** — Governance can freeze contracts, but unfreezing requires the Timelock (separation of powers)

**Key benefits:**
- Decentralized decision-making
- Separation of powers eliminates a single point of failure
- Emergency stop mechanism with protection against abuse

---

### No voting mechanisms

**Problem:** In traditional systems, users have no way to influence the development of the platform. All decisions are made centrally, without considering the opinion of the community.

**How Slices solves it:** The DAO provides a full-fledged voting system:
- **Creating proposals** — any staker with ≥1,000,000 PlatformToken can create a proposal
- **Voting** — lasts 7 days, requires a quorum of 40% of the total amount of staked tokens
- **Staking** — users stake PlatformToken, gaining voting rights and a 10% annual yield with automatic reinvestment
- **Timelock** — approved proposals take effect after 2 days, giving the community time to react

**Key benefits:**
- Democratic governance of the platform
- Incentives for long-term participation (10% annual yield)
- Protection against hasty decisions (Timelock)
- Transparency of all votes

---

| Governance problem | Slices solution |
|--------------------|----------------|
| Centralization | DAO with PlatformToken staker voting |
| Single point of failure | RBAC (AddressBook) with role separation |
| No voting | Proposals + 7-day voting + 40% quorum |

## 4. Who the platform is for

### Owners of real-world assets (real estate, businesses)

**Description:** The platform is intended for businesses seeking capital against real-world assets:

- **Growth companies** — established businesses seeking expansion capital (for example, a manufacturing company opening a new shop). For them — shorter completion periods, low risk scores, more frequent outgoing tranches.
- **Startups** — early-stage ventures with high growth potential (for example, a tech startup developing a new product). For them — longer periods, high risk scores, high reward interest rates.
- **Franchises** — franchise operations expanding to new locations (for example, a restaurant chain opening a new branch). Moderate periods and rates.

**How Slices helps:**
- Fast fundraising through asset tokenization
- Automatic distribution of capital through outgoing tranches
- Transparent reporting to investors through the blockchain
- AI assessment helps build investor trust

**Key benefits:**
- Access to a global pool of investors
- Minimal bureaucracy
- Flexible financing terms

---

### Investors (retail and institutional)

**Description:** The platform suits different categories of investors:

- **Retail investors** — anyone can deposit HOLD tokens into pools and receive RWA tokens representing a share in a real asset. Low entry threshold, diversification opportunities.
- **Large investors** — can participate in governance through the DAO, create proposals (≥1,000,000 PlatformToken required), and influence the development of the platform.
- **Stakers** — users who stake PlatformToken to gain voting rights and a 10% annual yield with automatic reinvestment of rewards.

**How Slices helps:**
- Access to investments in real-world assets with a low entry threshold
- Exit an investment at any time through the AMM
- Transparent information about risks (AI Risk Score)
- Additional income through staking and the referral program

**Key benefits:**
- Democratic access to RWA
- Liquidity through the AMM
- Income from staking (10% annual yield)
- Referral bonuses
