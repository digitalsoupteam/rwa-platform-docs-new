# FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

## About the Platform

### What is Slices?
Slices is a platform that turns real-world assets (for example, a business, real estate, or a startup) into digital tokens. Anyone can invest in these assets through dedicated pools that work like crowdfunding. Everything runs on the blockchain, but you do not need to be a technical specialist — the interface is simple and clear.

### What problems does Slices solve?
- **Access to investments**: previously, only large investors could put money into a real business; now it is available to everyone.
- **Transparency**: all operations are recorded on the blockchain and can be verified.
- **Liquidity**: you can sell your tokens at any time through the built-in exchange (AMM).
- **Governance**: holders of the platform token (RWAP) can vote on important changes.

### How do I start using the platform?
1. Connect a wallet (for example, MetaMask) to the BSC Testnet network.
2. Get test tokens from the faucet — BNB for gas, HOLD for operations, RWAP for governance.
3. Choose a pool to invest in, or create your own RWA token.

## Tokenization

### How do I create my own RWA token?
1. Register a company and a business on the platform.
2. Complete the AI risk evaluation (automatic).
3. Wait for multi-signature approval (several signers have to confirm).
4. Pay the fee in HOLD tokens.
5. Deploy the RWA contract and the pool through the Factory.

### How much does it cost to create an RWA token?
The RWA creation fee is 100 to 10,000 HOLD (depending on the asset type). The pool creation fee is 1% to 10% of the target raise amount. All fees are charged in HOLD.

### What types of assets can be tokenized?
- **Growth** — a growing business.
- **Startup** — an early-stage startup.
- **Franchise** — a franchise.
Each type has its own risk parameters, terms, and fees.

## Pools (Investments)

### How do I invest in a pool?
Choose a pool and click "Buy" (Mint). Specify how much HOLD you want to invest. The system calculates how many RWA tokens you will receive. Confirm the transaction in your wallet.

### How do I withdraw money from a pool?
Sell your RWA tokens back to the pool with the "Sell" (Burn) button. You receive HOLD based on the current price and bonuses (if the business returned more than it raised). The exit fee is 1–5%.

### What are the risks?
- **Business risk**: if the business does not return the funds, you may lose part of your investment.
- **Liquidity risk**: you can always sell tokens in the pool, but the price may be lower than you expected.
- **Platform risk**: smart contracts may have bugs, although they are audited and have protections (multi-signature, pause).

### What are tranches?
Tranches are scheduled payouts to the business (outgoing) and returns from the business (incoming). The business receives funds in parts, and investors receive returns in parts as well. There are fixed and floating tranches (flexible terms).

### What are bonuses?
If the business returns more than was raised, the surplus is distributed among investors in proportion to their share. This is additional profit.

## DAO and Governance

### How do I vote on proposals?
1. Get RWAP tokens (PlatformToken).
2. Stake them in the DaoStaking contract — 1 RWAP = 1 vote.
3. When a proposal appears, vote "For" or "Against". Your tokens are locked until the voting ends.
4. If the proposal reaches quorum (40% of all RWAP) and gets more "For" votes, it is executed automatically.

### How do I get PlatformToken (RWAP)?
On the testnet — through the faucet. On the mainnet — buy it on an exchange or receive it from the platform (for example, for activity). RWAP can also be earned by taking part in the loyalty program.

### Who can create proposals?
Anyone with at least 1,000,000 RWAP staked. Voting lasts 7 days.

## HOLD Token

### What is HOLD?
HOLD is the platform's main utility token. It is used for:
- paying fees (creating RWAs, creating pools, entering and exiting pools);
- buying RWA tokens in pools;
- receiving bonuses.
On the testnet, HOLD is a simulation of USDT (a stablecoin). On the mainnet it will be real USDT on the BSC network.

### How do I get HOLD on the testnet?
Use the faucet on the platform website. You will receive test HOLD, BNB (for gas), and RWAP. The faucet gives out tokens once every 24 hours.

## Fees

### How many fees are there and how are they calculated?
There are 4 types of fees (all in HOLD):
1. **RWA creation** — a fixed amount (100–10,000 HOLD).
2. **Pool creation** — a percentage of the target amount (1–10%).
3. **Entering a pool (buying)** — a percentage of the purchase amount (1–5%).
4. **Exiting a pool (selling)** — a percentage of the sale amount (1–5%).
Fees go to the treasury (Treasury), which the DAO manages through a Timelock (2-day delay).

## Security

### How are my funds protected?
- **Multi-signature**: creating an RWA or a pool requires approval from several independent signers.
- **Upgradeable contracts**: smart contracts can be upgraded, but only through a DAO vote and with a delay.
- **Emergency pause**: in case of an attack, the DAO can pause the contracts.
- **Timelock**: all treasury withdrawals have a 2-day delay so the community can react.
- **Roles**: different participants have different permissions (administrator, manager, signer, staker).

### What is multi-signature?
This is a mechanism where an important operation (for example, creating a token) requires several signatures from different people. This prevents abuse.

## Technical Questions

Currently — BSC Testnet (Chain ID 97). A mainnet launch on BSC is planned for the future.

### Where can I find the contract addresses?
All addresses (proxies and implementations) are listed in the documentation section "Smart Contracts → Deployments".

### Is there an API?
Yes, the platform provides a GraphQL API. Access requires a JWT token obtained by signing with a wallet. API keys will be added in the future.

### How do I get test tokens?
Use the faucet on the website. It gives out BNB, HOLD, and RWAP once every 24 hours.

### Does the platform work on mobile devices?
Yes, the interface is responsive, but you will need a mobile wallet (for example, MetaMask Mobile) to sign transactions.

### What should I do if I run into a problem?
Ask the AI assistant on the platform — it answers questions in chat. You can also contact support.
