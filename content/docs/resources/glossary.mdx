# Glossary

## RWA (Real World Asset)

A real-world asset tokenized on the blockchain in ERC-1155 format. An RWA token represents a share in a real business (Growth, Startup, or Franchise) and trades through liquidity pools with a built-in AMM.

→ [Tokenization](../tokenization/overview)

---

## Tokenization

The process of converting rights to a real-world asset into a digital token on the blockchain. It includes creating a company and a business, deploying the RWA contract through the Factory, configuring the pool, and issuing tokens.

→ [Tokenization](../tokenization/overview)

---

## AMM (Automated Market Maker)

An algorithm that determines the token price based on the constant product formula (x·y = k). It is built directly into every liquidity pool, allowing RWA tokens to be bought (mint) and sold (burn) without a traditional order book.

→ [Order Matching](../trading/order-matching)

---

## Liquidity Pool

A crowdfunding-type smart contract that accumulates funds (HOLD) and issues RWA tokens. It contains a built-in AMM, supports tranches and bonuses, and goes through several lifecycle stages: Entry Period → Trading → Completion → Fully Returned → Bonus Unlock.

→ [Pools](../pools/overview)

---

## Tranche (outgoing/incoming)

- **Outgoing Tranche** — a payment from the pool to participants (return of the invested principal + yield).
- **Incoming Tranche** — a payment from the business into the pool (funds received from operating activity).

Tranches can have fixed or floating payout dates.

→ [Tranches & Distributions](../pools/tranches)

---

## HOLD Token

The platform's main stablecoin (ERC-20, non-upgradeable) with a fixed total supply of 1 quadrillion tokens. It is used for all operations: creating RWAs, funding pools, and paying fees. On the testnet it acts as a USDT mock.

→ [HoldToken](../smart-contracts/hold-token)

---

## PlatformToken (RWAP)

The platform's governance token (ERC-20, upgradeable). It grants voting rights in the DAO and is used for staking (10% APY), creating proposals, and voting. It differs from HOLD in that it is a governance token rather than a means of payment.

→ [PlatformToken](../governance/platform-token)

---

## DAO (Decentralized Autonomous Organization)

A decentralized autonomous organization — a system of platform governance through voting by holders of PlatformToken (RWAP). It includes mechanisms for creating proposals, voting, quorum, the Timelock controller, and treasury management.

→ [Governance](../governance/proposals)

---

## Governance

The platform's governance system, implemented as a set of smart contracts: Governance (voting), Timelock (execution delay), Treasury (treasury), DaoStaking (staking). It allows protocol parameters to be changed, upgrades to be performed, and funds to be managed through voting.

→ [Governance](../governance/proposals)

---

## Timelock

A controller for the time delay on the execution of DAO decisions. Once a proposal is accepted, it is not executed immediately but enters a queue with a delay (timelock delay), giving the community time to react. Managed through Governance.

→ [Proposals](../governance/proposals)

---

## Proposal

A proposal in the DAO system. Types: changing protocol parameters, upgrading contracts, treasury management, and signer management. It goes through a lifecycle: Created → Voting → Auto-Executed or Auto-Cancelled.

→ [Proposals](../governance/proposals)

---

## Quorum

The minimum turnout threshold for a decision to be made in the DAO. It is set at 40% of the total PlatformToken supply. If quorum is not reached, the proposal is automatically rejected.

→ [Voting](../governance/voting)

---

## Staking

A mechanism for locking PlatformToken (RWAP) in the DaoStaking contract to earn rewards (10% APY) and increase voting power. The longer tokens are locked, the higher the voting weight.

→ [Staking](../governance/staking)

---

## Voting Power

The voting strength of a PlatformToken holder. It depends on the number of tokens locked in staking and the lock duration. It determines a participant's voting weight on DAO proposals.

→ [Voting](../governance/voting)

---

## Multi-Signature (Multi-sig)

A mechanism that requires confirmation from several signers to perform critical operations: deploying an RWA token, deploying a pool, and withdrawing funds from the ReferralTreasury. It is used to improve security and provide distributed control.

→ [Signers Manager](../signers-manager/overview)

---

## Signer

A participant in the multi-signature scheme authorized to sign transactions on behalf of the platform. Managed through Governance. Executing an operation requires signatures from 3 of 5 signers (3/5 multi-sig).

→ [Signers Manager](../signers-manager/overview)

---

## UUPS (Universal Upgradeable Proxy Standard)

A standard for upgradeable smart contracts in which the upgrade logic resides in the implementation contract rather than in the proxy. It is used for all core platform contracts (except HoldToken). Upgrades are performed through the AddressBook.

→ [Upgradeable Contract](../smart-contracts/upgradeable-contract)

---

## EventEmitter

A system smart contract that aggregates and emits events from other platform contracts. It is used by the backend (Indexer Service) to track on-chain state changes.

→ [Smart Contracts Architecture](../smart-contracts/architecture)

---

## AddressBook

The central registry of all platform smart contracts. It stores proxy and implementation addresses and manages roles and permissions (RBAC). Contract upgrades via the UUPS standard are performed through the AddressBook.

→ [Smart Contracts Architecture](../smart-contracts/architecture)

---

## Factory (RWA Factory, Pool Factory)

Smart contracts for creating RWA tokens (RWA Factory) and liquidity pools (Pool Factory). Deployment is performed through the Factory with multi-signature confirmation. The Factory validates parameters and creates new contract instances.

→ [Token Creation](../tokenization/token-creation) · [Launch a Pool](../pools/launch-pool)

---

## Risk Score

A risk rating of a business or pool on a scale from 1 to 100, calculated by the AI Evaluator. It takes into account 10 factors, including the business type (Growth, Startup, Franchise), financial metrics, and other parameters. It affects the pool configuration and fees.

→ [AI Evaluator](../ai-evaluator/overview)

---

## AI Evaluator

A synchronous AI risk evaluator built on OpenRouter (model `google/gemini-2.0-flash-001`). It evaluates businesses and pools across 10 factors, returning a score of 1–100. It is built into the RWA Service and is used when creating pools.

→ [AI Evaluator](../ai-evaluator/overview)

---

## AI Assistant

A conversational AI assistant for supporting platform users. It runs on the `gpt-4o-mini` model via OpenRouter. It supports contextual modes (investor_base, product_owner_base, popular_pools, user_portfolio). Limits: 10 assistants per user, 4096 characters per message, 30 days / 100 messages of history.

→ [AI Assistant](../ai-assistant/overview)

---

## Testnet Faucet

A testnet faucet for distributing tokens on BSC Testnet: BNB (for gas), HOLD, and PlatformToken (RWAP). Available through GraphQL mutations (`requestGas`, `requestHold`, `requestPlatform`) with a limit of 24 hours between requests.

→ [Testnet Faucet](../testnet-faucet/overview)

---

## BSC (Binance Smart Chain)

The Binance Smart Chain network (Chain ID 97 for the testnet), on which all platform smart contracts are deployed. It provides EVM compatibility, low fees, and high throughput.

→ [Deployments](../smart-contracts/deployments)

---

## BSC Testnet

The Binance Smart Chain test network (Chain ID 97), used for developing and testing the platform. It contains deployed versions of all contracts with test tokens (BNB, HOLD, RWAP) available through the Faucet.

→ [Deployments](../smart-contracts/deployments)

---

## ERC-1155

An Ethereum multi-token standard used for RWA tokens. It allows a single contract to manage many different token types (token IDs), which is efficient for representing different real-world assets.

→ [Tokenization Overview](../tokenization/overview)

---

## ERC-20

The Ethereum fungible token standard. It is used for the HOLD token (non-upgradeable) and PlatformToken / RWAP (upgradeable, with the UUPS mechanism).

→ [HoldToken](../smart-contracts/hold-token) · [PlatformToken](../smart-contracts/platform-token)

---

## Grace Period

A grace period after a pool's fundraising period ends (Entry Period). During this time, participants can exit the pool without penalties if the target amount was not reached.

→ [Pool Lifecycle](../pools/lifecycle)

---

## Floating Timestamp

A mechanism for floating dates on tranches (outgoing tranches). Instead of a fixed payout date, it uses a condition: the tranche becomes available once a certain amount has accumulated in the pool. This allows for more flexible management of distributions.

→ [Tranches & Distributions](../pools/tranches)

---

## Virtual Reserves

An AMM mechanism in which part of a pool's reserves is considered "virtual" and does not participate in pricing. It is used to limit price impact and prevent manipulation of the RWA token price during large trades.

→ [Order Matching](../trading/order-matching)

---

## Bonus (awaiting bonus)

An additional reward for pool participants after all investments are fully returned (Fully Returned). The bonus is paid from the business's profit and remains in "awaiting" status (awaiting bonus) until the business generates sufficient profit.

→ [Pool Lifecycle](../pools/lifecycle)
