# Use Cases

The **Slices** platform provides a full cycle for tokenizing real-world assets (RWA),
raising capital through crowdfunding pools, and decentralized governance.
Five key use cases are described below.

---

## Use Case 1: Tokenizing a Business and Raising Capital Through a Pool

### Problem

The owner of a small business (furniture manufacturing) wants to raise **50,000 USDT** to expand
the workshop, but has no access to traditional bank lending. Investors are not ready
to commit large sums without liquidity and transparency.

### Solution on the Platform

Slices lets you tokenize the business as an **RWA asset** (ERC-1155) and create
a crowdfunding **Pool** with a built-in **AMM**, through which investors can buy
and sell shares in the business.

### Step-by-Step Scenario

1. **Company creation.** The owner registers a company on the platform, specifies the name,
   country, description, and social media.
2. **Business creation.** Inside the company, a business of type `growth` is created with a project
   description, financial metrics, and uploaded documents.
3. **AI Evaluation.** The platform automatically analyzes the business (documents, images)
   and assigns a **riskScore** (0–100) with detailed reasoning and factors.
4. **Multi-sig approval.** Authorized **Signers** sign an EIP-712 message
   to deploy the RWA contract through the **Factory**.
5. **Pool creation.** The owner configures the pool: `expectedHoldAmount = 50,000 HOLD`,
   entry fee 2%, exit fee 2%, bonus rate 10%, outgoing and incoming tranches.
6. **Pool multi-sig approval.** Signers sign the pool deployment again.
7. **Entry Period.** Investors buy RWA tokens through the AMM using the formula `k = x·y`.
8. **Target Reached.** The target is raised — the business receives the first **OutgoingTranche**.
9. **Trading.** RWA tokens trade on the built-in AMM; the business pays out
   **IncomingTranches** back into the pool on schedule.
10. **Fully Returned + Bonus Unlock.** All funds are returned, and bonuses are distributed
    among RWA holders.

### Result

- The business received 50,000 HOLD for development.
- Investors hold liquid RWA tokens that can be sold at any time.
- All fees (entry, exit, creation) go to the **Treasury** and are managed through the **DAO**.

## Use Case 2: Investing in RWA Pools with Portfolio Diversification

### Problem

An investor with 10,000 USDT wants to diversify their investments across several real-world
assets, but buying shares in each business separately requires large sums
and complex legal paperwork.

### Solution on the Platform

Slices provides **AMM pools** with fractionalized RWA tokens — an investor can buy
any number of tokens in each pool, starting with small amounts.

### Step-by-Step Scenario

1. **Wallet connection.** The investor connects MetaMask (BSC Testnet) and signs
   an EIP-712 message for authentication (JWT).
2. **Balance top-up.** The investor gets **HOLD** through the **Testnet Faucet** (or buys
   it on an exchange in production).
3. **Pool selection.** Through **Portfolio** and **Charts**, the investor reviews the available pools:
   - Pool A: riskScore 25, expectedHoldAmount 100,000 HOLD, entry fee 2%
   - Pool B: riskScore 55, expectedHoldAmount 50,000 HOLD, entry fee 3%
   - Pool C: riskScore 80, expectedHoldAmount 20,000 HOLD, entry fee 5%
4. **Buying RWA.** The investor buys RWA tokens in each pool through the AMM:
   - Pool A: 5,000 HOLD → receives RWA using the AMM formula
   - Pool B: 3,000 HOLD → receives RWA
   - Pool C: 2,000 HOLD → receives RWA
5. **Monitoring.** Tracks the RWA price through **Charts** (OHLC candles, real-time
   WebSocket priceUpdates).
6. **Selling.** If needed, the investor sells RWA through the AMM (burn) and pays the exit fee.
   If the pool has reached the **Bonus Unlock** stage, the investor receives a bonus on top of the sale amount.
7. **Referral program.** If the investor was invited by a referrer, part of the fees
   (buy/sell) is automatically sent to the **ReferralTreasury**.

### Result

- The investor holds a diversified portfolio of 3 RWA assets.
- All operations are on-chain, transparent, and instant.
- Income comes from the growth in the value of RWA tokens and bonuses when funds are returned.

## Use Case 3: Participating in the DAO — Staking and Voting on Protocol Parameters

### Problem

Active participants on the platform want to influence key protocol parameters:
fee sizes, pool limits, treasury distribution. Without a DAO, all decisions
are made centrally.

### Solution on the Platform

The **DAO**, based on the **PlatformToken (RWAP)** and **DaoStaking**, lets participants
lock tokens, gain voting power, and create/vote on proposals.

### Step-by-Step Scenario

1. **Getting PlatformToken.** A participant requests RWAP through the **Testnet Faucet**
   (in production — buying it or receiving it through Governance.mint()).
2. **Staking.** The participant goes to the DAO section and stakes 5,000 RWAP in **DaoStaking**.
   - Voting power: 5,000 (1:1 with the stake).
   - Annual staking reward: 10% (500 RWAP per year).
3. **Creating a proposal** (requires ≥ 1,000,000 RWAP staked). The participant creates
   a proposal to reduce the entry fee from 3% to 2% for all pools.
   - Description: rationale, links to analytics.
   - Voting period: 7 days.
4. **Voting.** Other participants with staked RWAP vote "for" or "against".
   - When voting, tokens are locked until the vote ends.
5. **Auto-execution.** After each vote, quorum is checked (40% of the totalSupply
   of RWAP). If quorum is reached and there are more "for" votes, the proposal is executed
   automatically.
6. **Timelock (2 days).** If the proposal affects the **Treasury** (for example, withdrawing
   funds), execution is delayed by 2 days through the **Timelock**.

### Result

- Participants govern the protocol in a decentralized way.
- Stakers earn passive income (10% per year in RWAP).
- All decisions are transparent and irreversible once executed.

## Use Case 4: Referral Program and Airdrop to Attract New Participants

### Problem

The platform needs to scale its user base. Traditional advertising is expensive,
and organic growth is slow. A loyalty program is needed to motivate participants
to bring in new investors and business creators.

### Solution on the Platform

A **loyalty program** with referral links, reward accrual in the
**ReferralTreasury**, and a potential **airdrop** based on scoring.

### Step-by-Step Scenario

1. **Getting a referral link.** A participant (the referrer) opens the Loyalty section
   and generates a unique referral link.
2. **Invitation.** The referrer shares the link on social media, in chats, or on a blog.
3. **Referral registration.** A new user follows the link and connects
   a wallet — the system records the link: referrer → referral.
4. **Referral activity.** The referral performs operations:
   - Buying RWA (entry fee) → the referrer receives a % in the **ReferralTreasury**
   - Selling RWA (exit fee) → the referrer receives a %
   - Creating RWA (creation fee) → the referrer receives a %
   - Creating a pool (pool creation fee) → the referrer receives a %
5. **Withdrawing rewards.** The referrer requests a withdrawal. This requires 3 of 5 **Signers**
   signatures (multi-sig) and a 30-day cooldown.
6. **Airdrop.** Based on the results of the period, the platform runs an airdrop:
   - **Criteria:** fee volume, number of operations, referral influence,
     token/pool creation.
   - **Composite scoring** determines the reward size for each participant.

### Result

- The referrer earns passive income from the fees of invited users.
- The platform grows organically through the referral network.
- The airdrop motivates long-term activity and loyalty.

## Use Case 5: Full Cycle — From Startup to Investment Return with Bonuses

### Problem

A green energy startup is looking for **200,000 USDT** for a pilot project.
Investors want not only the return of their principal but also a risk premium.
Traditional crowdfunding platforms do not provide built-in liquidity
and bonus mechanisms.

### Solution on the Platform

Slices offers a full cycle: tokenization → pool with tranches and bonuses →
AMM trading → return with a bonus.

### Step-by-Step Scenario

#### Phase 1: Preparation

1. **Company.** The startup registers a company on the platform and adds participants
   with different permissions.
2. **Business.** A business of type `startup` is created with a detailed business plan,
   financial forecasts, documents, and images in the **Gallery**.
3. **AI Evaluation.** The system analyzes the project, outputs riskScore = 45 (moderate
   risk), and lists positive/negative/neutral factors.
4. **Multi-sig.** Signers review and sign the RWA contract deployment.

#### Phase 2: Pool Launch

5. **Pool Configuration.** The pool is configured:
   - `expectedHoldAmount = 200,000 HOLD`
   - `entryFeePercent = 150 bps` (1.5%)
   - `exitFeePercent = 100 bps` (1%)
   - `rewardPercent = 3000 bps` (30% bonus)
   - OutgoingTranches: 50,000 HOLD immediately, 150,000 HOLD after 3 months
   - IncomingTranches: 50,000 HOLD after 6 months, 50,000 after 9 months,
     100,000 after 12 months, 60,000 bonus after 13 months
6. **Multi-sig.** Signers sign the pool deployment through the **Factory**.

#### Phase 3: Raising Funds

7. **Entry Period (30 days).** Investors buy RWA tokens through the AMM.
   - The entry fee (1.5%) goes to the **Treasury**.
   - The investors' referrers receive a % in the **ReferralTreasury**.
8. **Target Reached.** The target is reached on day 20.
   - Floating timestamps: all tranches shift 10 days earlier.
   - The first outgoing tranche (50,000 HOLD) is paid out to the startup.

#### Phase 4: Trading and Return

9. **Trading.** RWA tokens trade on the AMM. Investors can sell their share
   at any time by paying the exit fee (1%).
10. **IncomingTranches.** The startup returns the funds on schedule:
    - 6 months: 50,000 HOLD → the RWA price rises
    - 9 months: 50,000 HOLD → the RWA price rises
    - 12 months: 100,000 HOLD → all funds are returned
11. **Completion Period.** New purchases are blocked; sales remain possible.
12. **Fully Returned.** The pool moves to the fully returned status.
13. **Bonus Unlock.** The bonus (60,000 HOLD = 30%) is distributed among RWA holders
    in proportion to their share.

#### Phase 5: Post-Investment Period

14. **DAO.** RWAP holders can create a proposal to change the parameters
    of future pools based on the experience of this cycle.
15. **Loyalty.** Participants who brought referrals accumulate rewards
    and take part in the airdrop.

### Result

- The **startup** received 200,000 HOLD for development and returned it with a 30% bonus.
- **Investors** received: return of the principal + 30% bonus + the ability to exit
  early through the AMM.
- The **platform** collected fees in the Treasury, and referrers received rewards.
- The **DAO** gained experience to improve the protocol parameters.
