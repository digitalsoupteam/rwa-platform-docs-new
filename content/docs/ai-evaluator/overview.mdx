# AI Risk Evaluator

The AI Evaluator is an automated function built into the RWA service that analyzes businesses and pools on the Slices platform, assigning risk scores to help participants make informed decisions.

## How Evaluation Works

The evaluation is performed **synchronously** (via `async/await`) as a direct call to **OpenRouter** from within the RWA service. It is **not a separate microservice** — the logic lives inside `business.service.ts` and `pool.service.ts`.

```
Business/Pool Created → RWA Service → OpenRouter (google/gemini-2.0-flash-001) → Risk Score Saved
```

### Evaluation Types

The system supports two types of evaluations:

| Type | What It Evaluates | When Triggered |
|------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Business Evaluation** | A business on the platform | After business creation |
| **Pool Evaluation** | A crowdfunding pool | After pool creation |

## What Data Is Sent to OpenRouter

The evaluator sends the entity's profile data directly to the LLM:

### For Business Evaluation

| Data Source | What's Included |
|-------------|-----------------|
| **Business Profile** | Name, description, tags |

### For Pool Evaluation

| Data Source | What's Included |
|-------------|-----------------|
| **Pool Configuration** | Description, tags |

## Evaluation Process

The evaluation is a **single-stage** process:

1. The RWA service builds a system message containing the entity's data and a list of 10 risk factors (business model viability, market competition, regulatory compliance, financial stability, operational risks, management team, industry challenges, market positioning, technology potential, target market size).
2. The message is sent to OpenRouter as a `chatCompletion` request using the model `google/gemini-2.0-flash-001`.
3. The LLM returns a response in the format:

```
RISK_SCORE: [1-100]
REASONING: [text]
```

4. The RWA service parses the score, validates it (1–100), and saves it to the database.

## Risk Score

The risk score is an **integer between 1 and 100**:

| Range | Interpretation |
|-------|----------------|
| 1–33 | Low risk — well-established business, strong fundamentals |
| 34–66 | Medium risk — some concerns, moderate fundamentals |
| 67–100 | High risk — significant concerns, weak fundamentals |

## Updating Risk Score

Risk scores can be manually updated by authorized users via the [updatePoolRiskScore](/docs/api/rwa/pools/update-pool-risk-score) mutation (e.g., after additional due diligence). Provide the pool ID, new risk score, and optional reasoning.

## AI Model

The evaluator uses **OpenRouter** to access LLM models. The current model is **`google/gemini-2.0-flash-001`**, configurable per deployment.
