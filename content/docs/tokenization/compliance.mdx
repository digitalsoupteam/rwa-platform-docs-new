# Compliance

The Slices platform implements a multi-layered compliance system to ensure regulatory compliance and participant protection.

## Multi-Signature Approval

Every RWA token and pool deployment requires approval from authorized signers. This provides:

- **Identity verification** — signers verify the business owner's identity
- **Due diligence** — signers review the business before approving
- **Fraud prevention** — multiple independent approvals reduce the risk of malicious deployments

### Signer Roles

- **Authorized Signers** — a set of 3–5 trusted addresses (individuals or entities) designated by Governance
- **minSignersRequired** — the minimum number of signatures required for a deployment to be valid (typically 2–3, depending on the asset type and risk score)
- **Signer Independence** — each signer operates independently; no single signer can approve a deployment alone

### Approval Process

1. **Submission** — the business owner submits a deployment request via the platform, including all required metadata and documents
2. **Independent Review** — each authorized signer independently reviews the business profile, documents, and off-chain AI risk score
3. **Signing** — each signer who approves the deployment signs the deployment parameters (entity ID, contract address, fee configuration) with their private key
4. **Execution** — the deployment transaction is submitted on-chain with all collected signatures; the smart contract verifies that at least `minSignersRequired` valid signatures are present before executing

This multi-signature mechanism ensures that no single point of failure exists and that every deployment has undergone thorough human review, complementing the automated off-chain AI evaluation.

## Off-Chain AI Risk Evaluation

Before deployment, each business undergoes an off-chain AI-powered risk evaluation. The evaluation is performed entirely outside the blockchain — the AI model analyzes the business data, documents, and images on the platform's backend servers. The resulting risk score and factor breakdown are then passed to the smart contract as part of the deployment parameters.

- **Stage 1** — initial analysis of the business description
- **Stage 2** — deeper analysis incorporating documents and images
- **Risk score** — a numerical score (0-100) indicating the assessed risk level
- **Factors** — detailed breakdown of evaluation factors with impact ratings

The evaluation considers the business description, supporting documents, and uploaded images. Because the evaluation is off-chain, the AI model can be updated and improved without requiring smart contract upgrades, while the on-chain deployment always receives the latest risk assessment.

## Fees and Compliance

All fee parameters are bounded by Governance-configured ranges, ensuring that fees remain fair and predictable:

| Fee | Description | Compliance Aspect |
|---|---|---|
| **createRWAFee** | One-time fee in HOLD tokens paid when deploying an RWA contract | Prevents spam deployments; fee range is capped by Governance |
| **createPoolFeeRatio** | Percentage of the pool's target raise amount, paid at pool creation | Aligns pool creation costs with pool size; prevents oversized or undersized pools |
| **entryFee** | Percentage fee charged when a participant enters a pool (mints tokens) | Discourages short-term flipping; bounded to prevent excessive costs |
| **exitFee** | Percentage fee charged when a participant exits a pool (burns tokens) | Stabilizes the pool by discouraging rapid exits; bounded by Governance |

All fee values are validated against Governance-configured minimum and maximum ranges at deployment time. Any attempt to set a fee outside these ranges is rejected by the smart contract, providing a compliance safeguard against malicious or erroneous fee configuration.

## Automated Market Maker (AMM) and Compliance

The AMM uses the constant product formula to determine token pricing:

```
k = HOLD_reserve * RWA_supply
```

When a participant mints tokens, the required HOLD amount is calculated as:

```
HOLD_required = HOLD_reserve * (RWA_minted / (RWA_supply - RWA_minted))
```

### Compliance Implications

- **Price Manipulation Prevention** — the AMM formula ensures that large purchases or sales have a predictable, non-linear price impact. This makes it economically unfeasible to manipulate the token price through rapid buy/sell cycles, protecting participants from price manipulation attacks.
- **Transparent Pricing** — all AMM parameters (reserve balances, supply) are on-chain and publicly verifiable. Participants can independently verify that the price they receive matches the formula, eliminating information asymmetry.
- **Liquidity Requirements** — the AMM enforces that sufficient HOLD liquidity exists in the pool before tokens can be minted. This prevents pools from being created with insufficient backing, a key compliance safeguard.
- **fixedSell Alternative** — when `fixedSell` is enabled, the AMM is bypassed and tokens are sold at a fixed price. This mode is only available for pools that pass additional compliance checks, ensuring that only low-risk assets can use simplified pricing.

## Tranches (Outgoing / Incoming) and Compliance

Tranches define the payment schedule for a pool. They are structured as two arrays:

### Outgoing Tranches (Payments to Participants)

Each outgoing tranche has:
- **targetTimestamp** — when the payment is scheduled
- **amount** — total amount to distribute (in HOLD tokens)
- **distributed** — how much has been distributed so far

### Incoming Tranches (Contributions from Participants)

Each incoming tranche has:
- **targetTimestamp** — when the contribution is due
- **amount** — total amount expected
- **filled** — how much has been contributed so far

### Compliance Safeguards

- **Recipient Verification** — outgoing tranches can only be distributed to verified participant addresses. The smart contract checks that each recipient has completed identity verification before releasing funds, preventing payments to unverified or sanctioned addresses.
- **Temporal Restrictions** — tranche timestamps are validated against Governance-configured minimum and maximum durations. This prevents pools from having unrealistically short or excessively long payment schedules, protecting participants from predatory terms.
- **Proportional Distribution** — when a tranche is distributed, each participant receives a share proportional to their token holdings. The smart contract enforces this automatically, ensuring fair and equal treatment of all participants.
- **Audit Trail** — every tranche distribution and contribution is recorded on-chain, providing a complete, immutable audit trail for regulatory reporting and dispute resolution.

### How Participants Receive Payments

1. A participant holds RWA tokens in their wallet
2. When an outgoing tranche's `targetTimestamp` is reached, the pool's distributor calls `distribute()`
3. The smart contract calculates each participant's share based on their token balance relative to total supply
4. The participant can then claim their HOLD tokens via `claim()`

## Platform Controls

The protocol includes several safety mechanisms:

- **Emergency pause** — Governance can pause RWA contracts and pools in case of emergency. Note: unpause differs by contract type — RWA contracts require **Timelock** to unpause, while Pools can be unpaused directly by **Governance**.
- **Fee controls** — all fee parameters are bounded by Governance-configured ranges
- **Parameter validation** — all pool parameters are validated against Config limits at deployment
- **Timelock** — critical operations (treasury withdrawals, contract unpausing) are delayed

## Participant Responsibilities

Participants should:

- Review the business profile and risk score before contributing
- Understand that tokenized assets carry inherent risks
- Monitor pool performance through the platform's charts and portfolio tools
- Be aware that returns depend on the business fulfilling its obligations
